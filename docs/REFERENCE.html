Quick reference

mouSEE

Point at the screen with your eyes, using a normal webcam. A bubble shows where mouSEE thinks you're looking, and the cursor can follow it. Everything in calibration works hands-free: instructions are spoken, and you answer by looking right or left.

Action wheel

Look at one spot and the wheel opens around it. Then look at an option until its arc fills. While the wheel is open, your eyes pick options instead of moving the cursor.

1.0 slooking within 35 px of one spot opens it
0.9 son an option picks it
0.15 slooking away this long starts the option's timer over. A shorter glance keeps it
0.8 safter it closes, before looking can open it again

Opening it by looking needs the cursor following your eyes (Ctrl+Alt+M) and the Eyes only head mode, since the nudge modes already hold the bubble on a resting gaze. Ctrl+Alt+W works in every mode. The center and the empty slot pick nothing.

The yellow arc fills as you keep looking at an option.

Everyday use

  1. Start it with make run
    The first time, it asks whether to calibrate. Look right for yes.
  2. Look at the dot in the middle
    A quick re-center runs every start, so the bubble fits how you sit today.
  3. Check the status bar is green
    Bottom left. The bubble shows where you're looking.
  4. Press Ctrl+Alt+M to move the cursor
    Rest your eyes on a spot to open the action wheel.

Calibration

  1. Grid
    The logo moves to 20 places, 5 by 4. Look at each one while its ring fills.
  2. Head step
    16 dots while leaning left and right and tilting your head up and down. A live hint says how far.
  3. Check
    If dots got too few usable frames, it says why and where, then redoes the whole thing, then only the missed dots. Nothing is saved until it passes.
  4. Preview
    The bubble live with the new calibration. Keep it, or do the grid again.
  5. Eye check
    Close both eyes when asked, so it learns when your eyes count as closed.

Answering by looking

Every question in calibration works without hands. Hold your eyes on one side until its bar fills.

Right → Yes3 seconds
Left → No3 seconds
  • Look at the middle firstIt only counts after your eyes have been in the middle, so you read the question before answering.
  • Screens you look around onLike the grid's preview. Close your eyes for 1.5 s first to say you're done looking.
  • Use your eyes, not your headTurning your head can read as the opposite answer.

Make commands

From the project folder, in Git Bash. make has to be the MSYS2 one.

# first time
make setup
# the app with the camera window
make run
make run 720               # 360 · 480 · 720 · 1080
make run ARGS="--calibrate"
# tests, then the tracker on its own
make test
make tracker
make cameras               # which camera is which

Settings file

app/Settings/settings.json, made with the defaults on first run and never committed. Close mouSEE before editing it, or use the calibrations window where there's a control.

  • cursor.smoothing · cursor.sensitivitySmoothing on or off, and 0 (steadiest) to 1 (quickest). Try 0.25 if the bubble bounces.
  • head.modecancel · both · nudge, the three head modes in order.
  • camera.resolution · camera.fps1080p and 60 fps by default.
  • calibration.recenterOnStartThe quick re-center at every start.
  • Wheel timingsFixed for now, in WheelSettings. Ready to become settings when the settings menu is built.

If something's off

The bubble is a bit off everywherePress Ctrl+Alt+R to re-center. Still off: Ctrl+Alt+F for the accuracy test and fine-tune.
The bubble bouncesLower Sensitivity under Smoothing in the calibrations window. 0.25 is steady. Lower still holds stiller but follows small moves later.
It drifts when you move your headSit like you did during calibration, and follow the yellow where-to-sit hints. Tilting your head up or down throws off up/down the most.
Ctrl+Alt+M does nothingIt needs a calibration in use. Make one in the calibrations window (Ctrl+Alt+C). A fine-tune isn't required, but it makes the cursor land closer.
The wheel keeps opening by itselfIt opens after 1 s on one spot, in Eyes only. Switch to a head nudge mode to open it only with Ctrl+Alt+W.
Calibration says it needs another tryIt tells you why: face out of view, eyes closed or covered, or too few frames. Fix that and look right to try again.
Build fails with MSB3027mouSEE is still running and has the file locked. Close it with Ctrl+Alt+Q, then build again.
